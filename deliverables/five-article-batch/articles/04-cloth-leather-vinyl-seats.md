## Article

The right interior visit starts with the material you actually have. Cloth, leather, and vinyl do not take the same cleaner, and a product that is reasonable on one can smear or dry out another. Southern Auto Detailers needs that information for an in-shop interior appointment in San Antonio. This article does not give you a home recipe, and it does not claim that every mark will leave.

A general detailing sequence on DetailingWiki treats cloth and leather differently: cloth is wiped as fabric, while leather is associated with a leather cleaner and, separately, a conditioner. The guide is a broad workflow, not this shop’s menu, and it is old relative to any product you might buy tomorrow ([A guide in detailing steps](https://www.detailingwiki.org/guides/guide-detailing-steps/), listed as 2016). Use it only for the distinction. Follow the label of whatever product is actually chosen, and ask the shop what the visit includes.

### How to tell them apart without guessing

Cloth shows a weave. Liquid sits on the surface and then wicks in. Leather usually has a natural grain, and it can feel warm. Vinyl is a coated material. It can be molded to look like leather, including a fake grain, and it often feels more plastic once you look at the edge or the underside of a flap.

Look at the seat in daylight, from the side, not only from the driver’s door at night. Cloth shows threads. Leather grain is irregular. Vinyl grain is often more uniform, and the back of a loose flap can look like fabric-backed plastic. Write down what you see in the appointment request. A photo of the edge of the cushion is more useful than a product name. A wrong label leads to the wrong product. Perforated inserts, suede-like panels, and aftermarket covers are extra reasons not to guess from the driver’s seat alone.

### What the material changes

Cloth holds crumbs, sand, and drinks inside the pile. A surface wipe can look busy and leave the soil. That does not mean a particular machine or chemical is included in every interior detail. It means you should describe ground-in soil when you book, and ask whether it is in scope.

Leather is skin. Harsh degreasers and “make it shiny” dressings are a poor match. Conditioning, when it is appropriate, is not the same step as cleaning. The shop’s existing note on conditioning is [leather conditioning](https://southernautodetailers.com/blog/leather-conditioning-san-antonio). Read it as a discussion of leather, not as a claim that your seats are leather.

Vinyl can handle a different cleaner than leather and still be damaged by a solvent meant for tar or adhesive. A glossy dressing can also make the seat slick. If the goal is a clean seat, say that. If the goal is a dressed, shiny seat, say that too. They are not the same request.

### What not to do before the appointment

Do not soak a cloth seat with a household cleaner to “get a head start.” Do not use a saddle soap or a random oil on leather because a video did. Do not scrub a stain with a brush until the fabric pills. If you already tried something, say what it was. Hiding that makes the diagnosis worse.

Also say if a seat is torn, if the color is already worn at the bolster, or if a dye transfer from clothing is the complaint. Cleaning does not re-dye a panel. It does not rebuild a ripped seam.

### Bolsters, covers, and the seats you do not sit in

The outer bolster usually shows the wear, because that is where you slide in. Cleaning will not put grain back into leather that has been rubbed smooth, and it will not recolor vinyl that has faded. Seat covers hide the factory material and create a second surface: the cover might be fabric while the seat under it is leather. If the cover is staying on, say so. If you want the factory seat cleaned, the cover has to come off, and you should do that before you arrive if you can.

Rear seats and child-seat anchors collect crumbs even when the front looks fine. Mention them. A shop that only sees “the driver’s seat is leather” may not plan time for three rows. Aftermarket upholstery is another flag. If a shop installed the covers, you may know the material better than a glance suggests. Bring that information instead of a product name from a bottle under the seat.

None of this is a license to experiment the night before. A wet cloth seat driven to the studio can mildew in the foam. Leave the material dry, and describe what you see.

## Visible FAQs

### How do I know if my seats are leather or vinyl?

Look for a weave (cloth), a natural grain and a softer edge (often leather), or a plastic grain that may be vinyl. If you are unsure, say so.

### Can I use a leather conditioner on vinyl?

Not by default. Conditioner is a leather step. Vinyl needs its own product decision.

### Will interior detailing remove every stain?

No. Describe the stain and ask what is realistic. Old dye and soaked spills may remain.

### Does this include the carpets?

Only if that is part of the request. Seats and carpets are both cabin work, but you should say which one is the problem.
