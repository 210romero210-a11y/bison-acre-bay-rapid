## Article

Book the visit that matches the complaint. An interior detail is for the cabin. An exterior detail is for the paint, wheels, and outside glass. A full detail is both, in one appointment. It is not a code name for paint correction, and it is not a ceramic coating. Southern Auto Detailers can do those other jobs at the San Antonio studio, but they are quoted on their own when they are actually needed.

If the cabin is the problem and the paint is acceptable, an interior visit is the direct request. If the paint is filmed with pollen and the cabin is fine, book the exterior. If both the cabin and the paint are merely dusty, a full detail is still the right name, because splitting them into two trips costs you two drop-offs. If only one area is actually dirty, booking both so the invoice “sounds complete” is the wrong reason. The shop can tell you if the second half is a short add or a real second scope. Ask that before the deposit, not when you are standing in the bay. Guessing “the big package” wastes the day on work you did not need, or leaves out the work you did.

### What each name should mean

Use the service pages as the definitions, not a social-media menu.

- [Interior detailing](https://southernautodetailers.com/interior-detailing) is the cabin appointment.
- [Exterior detailing](https://southernautodetailers.com/exterior-detailing) is the outside appointment: a careful wash and the related exterior work described on that page.
- [Full detailing](https://southernautodetailers.com/full-detailing) combines those two scopes.

A full detail does not automatically include [paint correction](https://southernautodetailers.com/paint-correction) or [ceramic coating](https://southernautodetailers.com/ceramic-coating). Correction polishes clear coat. Coating is a protection product after the surface is ready. Either one can be discussed. Neither one is hiding inside the words “full detail.” The existing note on what a full visit contains is [what a full detail includes](https://southernautodetailers.com/blog/what-a-full-detail-includes).

This article also does not assume that every interior visit includes hot-water extraction, steam, disinfection, or odor treatment. Ask what will be done for your seats and carpets. If a stain or a smell may remain, you should hear that before the appointment, not after.

### A simple way to choose

Answer three questions.

1. What do you notice first when you open the door: the cabin, the paint, or both?
2. Are you unhappy with swirls in the sun, or only with dirt?
3. Do you want a protection product discussed, or only a cleaning visit?

Dirt in the cabin points to an interior. Dirt on the paint points to an exterior. Dirt in both points to a full detail. Lines in the clear coat point to a correction conversation, which is additional. A wish for longer-lasting protection points to a coating conversation, also additional, and only after the paint is acceptable.

### What to say when you request the visit

Say the material and the mess. Cloth seats, leather, or vinyl. Pet hair or sand. Wheels that have not been cleaned. A vehicle that lives outside. You do not need a product speech. You need the shop to block enough time.

The existing prep note is [how to prepare for a detail](https://southernautodetailers.com/blog/how-to-prepare-for-a-detail). Remove what you do not want in the shop. Do not expect a quote to survive a car that arrives full of tools if that was not mentioned.

### Hypothetical choices

These are examples, not jobs the shop has published.

- A commuter with a dusty dash and clean paint: interior only.
- A truck with brake dust and an acceptable cabin: exterior only.
- A family vehicle with sand in the carpets and pollen on the hood: full detail.
- Any of those with cobweb marks in the sun: add an inspection for correction. Do not assume the detail includes it.
- Any of those with a request to “ceramic coat it today” on neglected paint: stop and separate the coating quote from the cleaning quote.

### Time, and what cleaning does not include

A full detail takes longer because two scopes share one calendar slot, not because a secret correction step is tucked inside. If you need the car the same morning, say so. A rushed visit is how the wheels or the carpets get skipped. The site does not publish a fixed hour count, because a sandy SUV and a lightly used sedan are not the same job.

Cleaning also does not include bodywork, glass chip repair, or mechanical work. It does not include a promise about odor. Headlight haze, if that is the complaint, is its own topic on the existing site and should be named separately rather than assumed. If you want protection after the wash, ask whether the exterior visit stops at a clean, dry car or includes a wax or sealant. Coating remains a different appointment if the paint needs prep first.

## Visible FAQs

### Is a full detail the same as paint correction?

No. A full detail cleans the cabin and the exterior. Correction polishes paint and is its own decision.

### Does a full detail include a ceramic coating?

No. Coating is a separate service. Ask for it only if you want that quote.

### Can I book only the interior?

Yes. Use the interior page if the outside is not the problem.

### Will every stain or smell be gone?

Do not assume that. Describe them when you book and ask what is realistic.

### Do you detail at my house in Schertz?

No. You bring the vehicle to the San Antonio studio.
