# Request a detailing appointment

Use this skill when someone wants an in-shop appointment at Southern Auto Detailers in San Antonio, Texas.

## Facts

- Studio: 11692 Bricken Circle, San Antonio, TX 78233
- Phone: (210) 201-4847
- Email: contact@southernautodetailers.com
- Hours: Tuesday through Sunday, 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Closed Monday.
- The website form at https://southernautodetailers.com/contact sends a request. It does not reserve the time.
- A 20% non-refundable deposit of the quoted total holds the slot. The balance is due when the work is finished.
- The studio does not travel. There is no mobile detailing.

## Steps

1. Confirm the vehicle stays at the studio for the appointment.
2. Ask for vehicle size, condition, and whether the job is interior, exterior, a full detail, paint correction, or ceramic coating.
3. Tell the person to call (210) 201-4847 or send the form at https://southernautodetailers.com/contact.
4. Do not invent a price or promise a same-day time.
