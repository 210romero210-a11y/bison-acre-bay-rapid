## INTERIOR DETAILING CLUSTER MODE

Create ONE article that strengthens Southern Auto Detailers'
interior-detailing topic cluster.

Primary commercial hub:
https://southernautodetailers.com/interior-detailing

Business model:
Appointment-based, in-shop service at the confirmed San Antonio studio.
Do not create or imply travel-to-customer services, additional branches,
or standalone service packages that the business has not confirmed.

### 1. SELECT FROM THE INTERIOR CLUSTER ONLY

Eligible themes:

- Carpets and floor mats
- Embedded pet hair
- Stain assessment and cleaning limitations
- Cabin odors and cleaning limitations
- Cloth, leather, vinyl, and material identification
- Screens, controls, glossy trim, and sensitive surfaces
- Cleaning versus existing material damage
- Interior appointment preparation
- Confirmed quote factors and scheduling expectations
- Interior-only versus full-detailing decisions
- Maintaining the cabin between appointments

Do not select paint correction, coatings, exterior contamination,
or unrelated service topics as the primary subject.

### 2. CHECK EXISTING COVERAGE FIRST

Inspect the current blog index, interior service page, and relevant
existing articles.

Record:

- Existing article title and actual URL
- Primary reader question
- Relevant coverage
- Whether the candidate adds a distinct answer

Do not treat a different title or city modifier as a new topic.
If an existing article fully answers the candidate question, recommend
an update instead of creating a duplicate article.

Prioritize meaningful gaps before random selection.
If several equally suitable gaps remain, select one randomly using an
available randomization tool, or disclose that it is an editorial choice.

Use the topic ledger. Do not claim persistent memory beyond that file.
Do not repeatedly select a published or already generated article.

### 3. RESEARCH WITH MATERIAL-SPECIFIC CARE

Use DetailingWiki for technical background and original synthesis.

For sensitive surfaces, child restraints, product compatibility,
disinfecting claims, or potentially damaging cleaning procedures,
consult applicable manufacturer or authoritative instructions.

Do not infer that the shop uses a procedure merely because a source
describes it.

Do not invent permanent odor elimination, complete stain removal,
sanitization, mold remediation, repairs, or child-restraint services.

### 4. ASSIGN THE ARTICLE A CLUSTER ROLE

Choose one:

- Foundational explanation
- Problem/condition guide
- Material-care guide
- Service-selection guide
- Appointment-preparation guide
- Maintenance guide

State how its question differs from:

- The interior service page
- The closest existing article

### 5. IMPLEMENT RELEVANT INTERNAL LINKS

Include:

- A contextual link to the verified interior service hub
- One or two relevant existing sibling guides where useful
- A closing link to the verified contact/appointment destination

Add full-detailing or studio-location links only when they help answer
the article's question.

Use descriptive anchors suited to each destination.
Do not use identical commercial anchors repeatedly or follow an
exact-match percentage formula.

Anchor purpose:

- `/interior-detailing` evaluates the professional service. Examples:
  "interior detailing", "in-shop interior cleaning",
  "interior detailing in San Antonio".
- An educational spoke explains a problem or material. Examples:
  "cleaning carpets and floor mats", "embedded pet hair",
  "stain-removal limitations".
- `/contact` takes an action. Examples: "request an interior-detailing quote",
  "request a studio appointment".
- `/full-detailing` compares a broader service:
  "interior and exterior full detailing".
- `/san-antonio-78233` is arrival and location:
  "our Northeast San Antonio studio".

Do not use "book interior detailing" to link to a general educational article,
or "pet-hair removal service" to imply a standalone package.

Do not insert proposed, guessed, or unverified destinations as live links.
Proposed stain, odor, interior quote-factor, interior-versus-full, and
seat-material guides stay unpublished until a later request publishes them.
